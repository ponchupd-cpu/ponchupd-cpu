### 🚀 Featured Projects

<table>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### [🔍 OpsLens](https://github.com/ChunChun451/opslens)
A decision engine for customer-support operations. Finds why SLA deadlines are missed, predicts which open tickets will breach, assigns tickets with min-cost flow, and proves the impact in a discrete-event simulator.

- **8.79% vs 20.74%** missed promises against industry-standard routing (14 of 14 held-out days)
- Breach-risk AUC **0.973**, with TreeSHAP reasons per ticket
- Built for the **Deviathon 2.0 finale**, team SoyaChaap

`Python` `FastAPI` `LightGBM` `OR-Tools` `Postgres` `Redis` `Docker`

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### [🕶️ SmartGlass](https://github.com/ponchupd-cpu/smartglass)
A hands-free voice assistant for smart glasses. You speak, it looks at what the camera sees, and it answers out loud.

- **Hears:** records your voice from the mic, with automatic start/stop on speech
- **Sees:** uses Gemini vision to understand the camera frame together with your question
- **Speaks:** sends Gemini's answer to Deepgram TTS one sentence at a time, so the reply starts playing before it's finished

`Python` `Gemini API` `Deepgram` `asyncio` `sounddevice`
